Hi! I am a PhD candidate at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/english/), College of Education, and expect to graduate in June 2027. I am supervised by **Fan Ouyang** and co-supervised by **Wenzhi Chen**, **Shouling Ji**, and **Zhengwei Liu**.

I was a visiting PhD student at the [Science of Learning in Education Centre (SoLEC)](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/nie/science-of-learning-in-education-centre), National Institute of Education, Nanyang Technological University. During my visit, I was supervised by [Teo Wei-Peng](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DPN-tT0AAAAJ&hl=en) and co-supervised by [Dragan Gašević](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Q4Nm22sAAAAJ&hl=en).

My research interests include **Educational Technology (EdTech)**, **Computer-Supported Collaborative Learning (CSCL)**, **Multimodal Learning Analytics (MMLA)**, and **Educational Neuroscience**. I am particularly interested in understanding how emerging technologies—especially generative artificial intelligence—reshape learning processes and interactions.

I have expertise in **R programming**, **multimodal learning analytics**, **computational modeling**, and **educational neuroscience methods**. By integrating these complementary approaches, my research aims to:

1. design AI-supported collaborative learning environments;
2. develop multimodal and computational approaches to model and understand learning processes; and
3. uncover the cognitive and neural mechanisms underlying human–AI interaction (HAII).
